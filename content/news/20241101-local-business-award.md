The Swayzee EDC would like to extend its congratulations to Tammy Melton, owner of [Sassy Diva Sisters Boutique](https://www.facebook.com/SassyDivaSisterDesigns/) in Swayzee, who was given an Economic Impact Appreciation Award by the [Grant County Economic Growth Council](https://www.grantcounty.com/) during their [2024 Economic Impact Appreciation Awards and Local Elected Officials Dinner](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1GGbrfZLmz/) on October 30th. The award recognizes Grant Co. community members who have invested in the county's economic well-being and growth, and we are so proud to have Tammy and Sassy Diva Sisters in Swayzee! Congratulations, Tammy!

Sassy Diva Sisters Boutique is located at **108 S. Washington St., Swayzee, IN**, and is open **Noon-7pm, Wednesday through Saturday**, or browse [their online catalog](https://sassydivasisters.com/).
