The Swayzee EDC is offering engraved park benches with a family name inscription, placed at a park location of your choice.

A completed bench, the Watts family bench pictured above, is on display in front of the Town Clerk's Office.

The price is **$630**, including engraving. For the first 5 benches reserved, the Swayzee EDC will cover **$300**, so the final cost is **$330**.

If you're interested, please send a Facebook message to the Swayzee EDC at [facebook.com/onlyswayzee](http://facebook.com/onlyswayzee) by **January 15, 2027**.
