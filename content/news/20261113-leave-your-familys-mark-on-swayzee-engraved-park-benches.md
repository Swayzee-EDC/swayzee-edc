**Update, November 18, 2026:** Thanks to the Swayzee Town Council, 10 more benches are now available!

The Swayzee EDC is offering engraved park benches with a family name inscription, placed at a park location of your choice.

The response to the first round was wonderful, and every spot has been claimed. A big thank you to the Swayzee Town Council for opening up 10 additional benches and providing the match for this second round, just as the Swayzee EDC did for the first.

There are now 20 benches in total. 12 are spoken for, so **8 spots remain**.

A completed bench, the Watts family bench pictured, is on display in front of the Town Clerk's Office.

Each bench is **$600**, and the Town Council covers half (**$300**). The resident pays the other $300 plus **$30** for engraving, for a total of **$330**.

If you're interested, please send a Facebook message to the Swayzee EDC at [facebook.com/onlyswayzee](https://facebook.com/onlyswayzee) by **January 15, 2027**.

Learn more about the [Swayzee Town Council](https://www.in.gov/towns/swayzee/government/town-council/).
