Spring is just around the corner, and with the warmer weather comes the opportunity to 
get out of the house, see old friends, and enjoy our wonderful town. Looking for something 
to do on a (hopefully) sunny Saturday? Our local businesses are here to help!

On **Saturday, March 15th from 10am to 2pm**, [the Jungle Nook](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100093645439638)
will host their **Spring Pop-up Shop** at **[111 W Lyons Street](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7KeTbbsXaJu5MQjx6)**. 
Stop by to grab a cookie and some colorful greenery to brighten your home!

Then, on **Saturday, March 29th from 10am to 8pm**, visit the **Grand Opening** of 
[the Bigfoot Bake Stand](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61573974416760),
Swayzee's latest small business, at 
**[202 E Main Street](https://maps.app.goo.gl/g4PKwMbw71EpRA6r7)**. If you're 
looking for a fresh loaf of bread or just an afternoon snack, this self-serve 
bakery stand will feature a range of delicious breads, baked goods, and sweet treats!

We're extremely proud that our local businesses have decided to make Swayzee their home,
and we hope you'll join us in supporting them at these events!
