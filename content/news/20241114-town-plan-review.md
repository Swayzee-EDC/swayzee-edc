On Oct. 16th, [we met at the Swayzee Community Church](https://onlyswayzee.org/news/20241016-survey-next-steps/) to discuss the results of the town community survey and to get a glimpse of what our community envisions for itself 10 years from now. We are excited to announce that the Ball State team has now completed a full draft of the 10-year town plan and is ready to present it to the community for feedback! This will be the **final community meeting** where you can provide your input on the plan, and we want every voice to be heard.

Please join us for a **Town Plan Review Meeting** at the **Swayzee Community Church** on **Wednesday, November 20, 2024 at 6pm** to provide your feedback on the draft of the town plan!

**Nov. 20, 2024 at 6pm**\
**Swayzee Community Church**\
**306 South Washington Street**
