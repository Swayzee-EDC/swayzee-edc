Swayzee is bursting with pride. Cameron Balser, who grew up right here in Swayzee, has set a new record for running across America.

Cameron ran 3,067 miles from San Francisco City Hall to New York City Hall in **41 days, 16 hours, and 45 minutes**, finishing late Friday night, November 20, 2026. That's about 13 hours and 45 minutes faster than the previous record of 42 days, 6 hours, and 30 minutes, set by Pete Kostelnick in 2016. He started on October 10 and averaged about **73.6 miles a day**. He called the attempt "Breaking 42."

![Cameron Balser running along a country road during his record run](/images/news/cameron-road.jpg)

This isn't Cameron's first big run. In 2023 and 2024, he ran more than 11,000 miles around the perimeter of the United States, finishing that journey on the track at his alma mater, Oak Hill High School.

This time, Cameron also ran for a cause close to home, raising money for the Grant County Family YMCA in Marion, where he once worked. He crossed the finish line in New York wearing a YMCA of Grant County shirt.

Congratulations, Cameron. All of Swayzee is cheering for you!
