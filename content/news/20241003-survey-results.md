September was an exciting and busy month! 

On Sept. 11th, we hosted our first community forum in the Swayzee Town Hall, and we couldn't 
believe the turnout. We hoped that 50 people _might_ attend this meeting, but almost 70 of 
our wonderful community members packed themselves into the Town Hall in order to share their 
thoughts, ideas, and dreams for Swayzee. Dr. Michael Burayidi, our partner at Ball State 
University, told us that it was by far the most attended public forum he has led across all 
15 communities with which he has worked. We heard a lot of great thoughts during the forum,
but one thing came through loud and clear: the people of Swayzee care deeply about the future
of this town!

On Sept. 13th, we posted our first community survey, an opportunity to hear from even more
members of our community on what they love about Swayzee and what they wish could be improved.
Yet again, this community blew us away. After only 9 days, we received over 100 survey responses,
another record for the Ball State team. By the time the survey closed on Sept. 30, we had a 
total of **165 survey responses!** Thank you, Swayzee, for taking the time to complete the survey 
and help us envision the future of our beloved town.

But we're not done yet! On **October 16, 2024 at 6pm** we will be hosting a **survey results meeting**
at the **Swayzee Community Church** (formerly the Methodist church). Please join us to hear the 
Ball State team present the results from the survey:


**Oct. 16, 2024 at 6pm**\
**Swayzee Community Church**\
**306 South Washington Street**
