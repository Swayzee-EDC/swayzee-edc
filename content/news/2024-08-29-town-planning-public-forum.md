The Swayzee Economic Development Corporation (EDC) has partnered with Ball State University to design a 10-year town plan which will help guide our community's growth over the coming decade. When complete, this plan will define what our town wants Swayzee to look like in 2035 and will prioritize the improvements that must be made to reach that vision.

Ultimately, we want this 10-year plan to be an expression of our community, its priorities, and its heart. Over the coming months, the EDC and Ball State will be hosting a series of public forums and sending out a survey so that you, our community members, may provide your input on the plan.

Join us for the first public forum to help define our town's priorities for growth and revitalization:

**Sept. 11, 2024 at 6pm**\
**Swayzee Town Hall**\
**213 South Washington Street**
