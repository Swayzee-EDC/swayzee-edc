The Swayzee Economic Development Corporation has partnered with the USDA to provide low-cost financing options for local businesses and entrepreneurs who support the economic development of Swayzee. Currently, funds are limited with a total of $32,500 available on a first-come, first-served basis. For more information, fill out our [contact form](contact/) or call Don Thompson, president of the SEDC, at [(765) 661-2946](tel:765-661-2946).

### Fund Uses

* Land acquisition or improvement
* Building construction, acquisition, or improvements
* Renovations or restorations
* Purchase of equipment with a life of 7 years or longer

### Eligibility Requirements

* Must be a for-profit corporation, partnership, or proprietorship
* Located in Swayzee (or nearby)
* Create and/or retain jobs
* Have fewer than 50 employees
* Provide personal guarantees of the debt
