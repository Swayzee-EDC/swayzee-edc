The Swayzee EDC is dedicated to building Swayzee into a vibrant community with strong local businesses.
Learn more about our current programs and initiatives below.

### Revolving Loan Fund

Are you opening or currently operating a small business in or around Swayzee? Our **Revolving Loan Fund** provides 
low-cost financing options for local businesses supporting our town's economic development. 

[Learn more](/loans)

### Swayzee Farmers Market

Started in June 2025, the **Swayzee Farmers Market** provides fresh, local, and community-grown goods to Swayzee and 
the surrounding area. Now accepting applications for vendors!

[Learn more](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61574683337760)
