The Town of Swayzee is working with the Department of Urban Planning at Ball State University
to prepare a 10-year comprehensive plan for the town. As part of this process, we are seeking
resident feedback about Swayzee. We invite you to take the following survey which will take
less than 10 minutes to complete. 
**All responses will be confidential, and no personal information will be revealed.**

Please complete the survey using one of the following methods by **Monday, Sept. 30, 2024**:

#### Method 1: Fill out the online survey (recommended)
