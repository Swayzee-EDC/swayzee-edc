**Swayzee, IN** is a friendly town in northeast Indiana with easy access to 
Kokomo to the west and Marion to the east. Lying along Indiana SR 13 near its 
junction with U.S. Route 35, Swayzee is conveniently situated within an hour of 
both Indianapolis and Fort Wayne. Come see for yourself all that Swayzee has to 
offer!

## The Swayzee EDC
Informally launched in February 2023 by a group of citizens who wanted to 
assist the town board in improving the community, the 
**Swayzee Economic Development Corporation (EDC)** was founded to encourage 
Swayzee's economic and community development through the retention, expansion, 
and attraction of local businesses.

The Swayzee EDC was incorporated as a 501(c)(3) non-profit in August 2023, 
making us eligible for a large number of public and private grants. Our members 
meet monthly in the Swayzee Town Hall, and we are currently working to identify 
grants and development programs that are right for our town. 

Have an idea for us or want to know how to get involved? Fill out our 
[contact form](contact/) to let us know!
